My story

I'm on a mission to connect wisdom and tech.

My childhood and youth was defined by great friends, nature and video games. My 90s dream of "working with computers" became hyper real when I became the regional manager for Twitch in DACH at age 22. At Twitch I helped close to 1000 content creators become successful entertainment professionals and business owners. Some are making a solid living doing what they love, some are now superstars of the German speaking entertainment world. Today I work with founders and mission driven operators in the worlds of tech, art and entertainment.

The approach then was the same as it is today: Speak to creatives and builders about their sense of ownership when it comes to their work.

What do you want? What's the thing you're uniquely positioned to change in the world? How can you lend your voice to that?

My journey is that of a life long student. I have deeply studied creativity, productivity, identity transformation, relationship & community building, as well as various holistic health matters.

I've changed my own life many times over, and I love helping other people change theirs.

Knowledge is cheap now. Wisdom is rare. Grow bigger. Make your life count!
